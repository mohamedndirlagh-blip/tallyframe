FAQ

Plain answers

Which data sources can you use?

Anything you can give view access to or export: GA4, Google Ads, Google Sheets, and CSV exports from ad platforms and community tools. On-chain figures work when you can export them to a sheet. If a source cannot be exported, we tell you before starting.

Do you filter out bots and fake wallets?

No. The dashboard shows the figures your sources report. If your analytics tool already separates real wallets from bots, we chart the filtered number and label it. We will not present a count as verified when it is not.

Who builds the dashboard?

The build is done with AI and checked by a person before you see it. That is how a fixed price and a 48-hour turnaround are possible.

Does it update by itself?

Sources connected directly, such as GA4 and Google Ads, refresh on their own. Sources that arrive as exports update when you replace the file, and the guide shows how.

What do you do with our client's data?

We use it to build your dashboard and nothing else. Access can be view-only, and you can remove it as soon as the dashboard is delivered.

Why USDT only?

It holds a steady dollar value and settles in minutes from any country, which keeps a small fixed-price job simple for both sides.
